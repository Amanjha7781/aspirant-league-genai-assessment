PARKING POLICY (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE)

Visitor parking
Visitors may park in spaces marked VISITOR from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. A dashboard permit is required. The permit can be collected from the front desk.

Overnight parking
Overnight parking is not permitted in the visitor lot. Residents may use the east garage overnight with a valid resident pass.

Electric vehicle charging
The two charging spaces are beside the east garage entrance. Charging is limited to four hours. The facility team checks charging equipment each Monday.
